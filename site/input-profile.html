04 / DAILY STUDY ARCHIVESEOUL METRO · 2025

EPOCH 1 / RESEARCH NOTE

An earlier
daily-demand target.

Before the hourly forecast, the project explored next-calendar-day daily demand for each station, line and boarding/alighting direction. This archive documents that separate modeling question.

TARGET

One daily total per series

The earlier study predicts next-day total demand for a station × line × direction series, rather than 18 hourly outputs.

INFORMATION SET

History before the target day

The daily experiment uses past demand and calendar context with chronological validation, keeping future target counts out of the inputs.

WHY IT MATTERS

Different scale and metric

Its passenger errors are for daily totals and should not be compared directly with the hourly model's MAE.

For the current 18-hour forecast, see the featured study. It uses a 56-day history, a four-week same-hour median anchor and a separate final test.
