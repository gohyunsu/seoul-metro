03 / STATIONSSEOUL METRO · 2025

EPOCH 1 / RESEARCH NOTE

A network of
different places.

Station totals vary sharply. The data describes station, line and direction counts, which can reveal scale and routine without revealing individual routes.

COVERAGE

243 stations

The audited source spans Seoul Metro Lines 1–8. Line-station-direction combinations form 546 forecast series.

CONCENTRATION

Top ten: 15.4%

The ten busiest stations account for 15.4% of annual network volume in the exploratory study.

INTERPRETATION

No individual journeys

A boarding or alighting count cannot reconstruct a passenger's route. Station numbers are identifiers, not numeric distance measures.

Spatial comparisons should be read as descriptive counts. They do not by themselves explain why demand differs between stations.
