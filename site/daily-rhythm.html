02 / EXPLORATIONSEOUL METRO · 2025

EPOCH 1 / RESEARCH NOTE

The shape of
an ordinary day.

The year-long record has a distinct weekday rhythm. This exploratory view concerns observed demand; it is separate from the forecast model's held-out evaluation.

WEEKLY CONTRAST

49.6% higher on weekdays

Average daily network ridership on weekdays exceeded the weekend mean by 49.6% in 2025.

PEAK HOUR

18:00—19:00

Among the source file's 20 time bands, the evening 18–19 band had the greatest annual ridership.

READING THE CHART

Average, not live demand

The overview chart compares mean daily network counts by hour and day type; it is not a real-time crowding display.
