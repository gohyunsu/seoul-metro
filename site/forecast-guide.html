05 / DAILY STUDY ARCHIVESEOUL METRO · 2025

EPOCH 1 / RESEARCH NOTE

What the daily
baseline taught us.

The daily-target experiment retained a seven-day seasonal-naive forecast after validation. Its result belongs to the earlier daily study, not the newer hourly forecast.

SELECTED METHOD

Previous week's value

The seven-day seasonal-naive baseline was selected on validation for the next-calendar-day daily target.

FINAL TEST

28 Nov — 31 Dec 2025

On this daily-target test period, the selected baseline reached MAE 1,008 passengers and WAPE 5.9%.

INTERPRETATION

Keep targets separate

Daily totals and hourly demand have different units of comparison. The 71.11 passenger MAE belongs to the newer hourly study.

The newer hourly work uses a four-week same-hour median anchor and reports results on 15 November through 31 December 2025.
