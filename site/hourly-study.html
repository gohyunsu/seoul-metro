FEATURED STUDY / 2025NEXT-DAY HOURLY FORECAST

546 SERIES × 18 HOURS

Forecasting tomorrow,
one hour at a time.

Using 2025 ridership records for Seoul Metro Lines 1–8, we forecast demand from 06:00 to 23:00 for every line, station and boarding/alighting direction. The design retains a strong weekly baseline and learns only the correction it needs.

TESTED TARGETS18 regular hours
THE RESEARCH QUESTION

Can a model improve tomorrow's hourly forecast without discarding the weekly pattern already captured by a four-week median?

01 / RESEARCH DESIGNFix the information available at forecast time

From 56 days of history
to tomorrow's 18 hours.

Each of 546 line, station and direction series receives 18 regular hourly outputs. Irregular before-06:00 and after-24:00 aggregates are excluded from the forecast target.

01 / HISTORY

Observed demand

We use the strictly previous 56 days and explicit same-hour lags of 1, 7, 14, 21, 28, 35, 42, 49 and 56 days.

02 / IDENTITY & TIME

Known context

Station, line, direction, service hour, weekday and day of year are available before prediction. Station codes are treated as categories.

03 / OPTIONAL WEATHER

Issued forecasts only

Weather inputs are limited to forecasts issued by 05:10 KST on the target day. Realized target-day weather is never an operational input.

FIXED ANCHOR

The baseline is the median of the same hour 7, 14, 21 and 28 days earlier. The neural model starts with exactly zero correction and learns a bounded residual.

02 / EVALUATIONSelection and final assessment are separate

Choose on validation.
Report on held-out time.

All predictors respect the target date. We select the configuration with ordinary-day and special-day validation, then report its performance on the final period of 2025.

03 / INTERPRETATIONKeep the result in context

A better result.
Questions still open.

The selected run beat the four-week median on the same final test period. A single-year result does not establish stability in future operating conditions.

01 / WEATHER

Weather gains were inconsistent.

Forecast weather helped the causal base model, but worsened the special-day model when combined with it. We do not claim weather is uniformly helpful.

02 / SELECTION

Stress validation is not a terminal split.

Selected special days from August and October inform configuration choice. Without a separate multi-year evaluation, selection bias remains possible.

03 / GENERALIZATION

Beyond 2025 is untested.

Long-term drift, station changes and new service patterns require multi-year external testing and repeated forecast-origin evaluation.
